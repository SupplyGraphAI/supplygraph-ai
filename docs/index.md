# SupplyGraph AI — Agentic Supply Intelligence Platform

**SupplyGraph AI** is an AI-native, graph-based intelligence platform for global supply chains, trade compliance, and multi-tier risk analysis.

Designed for enterprises, researchers, and developers who need to monitor and act on complex global supply networks — **without requiring proprietary data uploads.**

## Core Capabilities

✅ Multi-tier (10+ levels) supply chain intelligence  
✅ AI-driven tariff & HTS classification  
✅ Trade compliance and duty impact analysis  
✅ Country & regional concentration risk detection  
✅ Alternative supplier discovery  
✅ Auditable, explainable outputs
