# SupplyGraph AI — Agentic Supply Intelligence Platform

**SupplyGraph AI** is an AI-native, graph-based intelligence platform for global supply chains, trade compliance, and multi-tier risk analysis.

It connects **products, companies, countries, policies, and risk signals** into a continuously updated, auditable supply graph — enabling organizations to **see, analyze, predict, and act** on complex international dependencies **without requiring proprietary data uploads**.

This site serves as the **official documentation portal** for:

- SupplyGraph AI Agents  
- The Agent-to-Agent (A2A) Protocol  
- Integration patterns & SDKs  
- Enterprise & research use cases  
- Live, production-grade agent demos

## 🧠 What Makes SupplyGraph AI Different

Traditional supply-chain tools rely on static data, Tier-1 visibility and manual supplier lists.

SupplyGraph AI introduces a **living, autonomous, multi-tier global supply graph** powered by intelligent agents, capable of:

✅ Multi-tier (10+ levels) supply chain intelligence  
✅ AI-driven tariff & HTS / HS classification  
✅ Trade compliance & duty impact analysis  
✅ Geographic & regional concentration risk detection  
✅ Single-country over-dependency identification  
✅ Supplier & region substitution discovery  
✅ Auditable, explainable outputs with evidence trails  
✅ No customer data or supplier list uploads required  

SupplyGraph AI reveals **hidden dependencies, bottlenecks, and exposure points** across the global economy using autonomous, graph-based AI agents.

## ⚙️ How SupplyGraph AI Works

1. SupplyGraph AI continuously builds and updates a **global, multi-tier supply graph**
2. You send a natural-language or structured request to an agent
3. The agent performs:
   - Entity recognition
   - Multi-tier graph expansion
   - Policy & tariff interpretation
   - Dependency scoring & risk modeling
   - Evidence validation
4. Results are delivered as:
   - Structured JSON
   - Visualized graphs
   - Actionable intelligence

Core architecture components:

- Graph Intelligence Layer  
- Multi-Agent Reasoning Layer  
- Policy & Trade Intelligence (HTS, Chapter 99, Sanctions)  
- A2A & SDK Integration Layer
